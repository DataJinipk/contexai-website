02Regional Market & Economic Matrix

A. Local Market — Pakistan Focus

Tuesday's session saw the KSE-100 retreat 0.63% to 177,083.22, shedding 1,116.80 points as institutional profit-taking followed Monday's 1.20% surge to 178,200.02. Breadth turned negative — 295 decliners against 161 advancers on 711.3 million shares (Rs25.9bn) — with Trading Economics confirming the pullback. Meezan Bank, PSO, Kapco, Power Cement and Millat Tractors added a combined 175 points, but UBL, Engro Holdings, OGDC, Lucky Cement and PPL erased 580 points on selective profit-taking in banks and E&P names. On the macro front, headline CPI eased to 9.2% YoY in July from 11.1% in June, giving the SBP room to hold its policy rate at 11.5% for a second straight meeting. Six-month KIBOR is tracking near 11.70–11.90% (EST.). The IMF's Executive Board cleared the third EFF/RSF review in May, unlocking roughly $1.2bn; the next mission, likely covering an Article IV consultation, is expected in H2 2026.

B. GCC & Middle East

Gulf oil producers added to August supply as seven OPEC+ members agreed to raise output by 188,000 bpd, split across Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria and Oman, continuing the unwind of 2023's voluntary cuts. That extra supply landed just as Brent slid 5.3% to a three-week low of $79.36 on hopes for a reopened Strait of Hormuz — a double-barrelled bearish setup for crude that nonetheless keeps the Gulf's non-oil diversification story intact. Saudi Arabia's non-oil PMI held at a still-robust 53.1 in July (53.3 in June), while the UAE's PMI eased to 55.8 from 57.1 — its slowest pace in three months, though new export orders hit a three-year high as demand broadened across the GCC and Europe, a supportive signal for Vision 2030-linked non-oil diversification.

C. Global Intelligence

Wall Street closed at fresh records on Tuesday — the S&P 500 jumped 1.79% to 7,736.52, the Dow added 907 points (+1.71%) to close above 54,000 for the first time, and the Nasdaq surged 2.59% to 26,584.99, powered by a tech rebound, strong earnings, and easing US-Iran tensions over Hormuz. Bonds told a more cautious story: the 10-year yield sits near 4.63% and the 2-year at 4.21%, with markets pricing roughly a 65% chance the Fed hikes rather than cuts in September after Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish-sounding hold on 29 July rattled the inflation-credibility narrative. Separately, Washington's trade agenda intensified: a 50% ad valorem tariff on Canadian goods takes effect 19 August, alongside Section 301 forced-labor duties of 10–12.5% on roughly 60 economies that took effect 24 July.
