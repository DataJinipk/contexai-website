02Regional Market & Economic Matrix

A. Local Market — Pakistan Focus

The KSE-100 surged 1,646.81 points (0.98%) to close at 170,511.85 on Friday, clawing back roughly half of Thursday's 3,079-point rout after reports of a planned Iran-GCC meeting on the Strait of Hormuz lifted sentiment; volume jumped to 687.47 million shares (Rs33.70bn) with 248 of 500 names advancing. That relief now looks fragile: the State Bank's Monetary Policy Committee meets today and is widely expected to hold the policy rate at 11.5% given August CPI at 11.15% y/y and 6-month KIBOR near 11.87%, while USD/PKR interbank was broadly steady around 277.35-277.55. On policy, the Sovereign Wealth Fund (Amendment) Bill, 2026 remains before the Senate Standing Committee on Finance and Revenue ahead of the IMF's fourth EFF/third RSF review mission, expected in the coming weeks and widely flagged around 22-23 September. Sources: Business Recorder, Business Recorder.

B. GCC & Middle East

Gulf oil markets swung sharply into the weekend: Brent eased to $104.61/bbl and WTI to $100.05 on Friday as an Oman-hosted summit between Iran and the GCC on reopening the Strait of Hormuz raised de-escalation hopes, only for Oman to postpone the talks indefinitely late Sunday after Bahrain declined to attend. Compounding the supply risk, Saudi Arabia's 7m-bpd East-West pipeline — its main bypass for roughly 5m bpd of crude that would otherwise transit Hormuz — remains shut following 10-11 September drone strikes on pump stations near Riyadh and Medina, itself days after Houthi strikes wounded 73 and disrupted energy sites including the 400,000-bpd Jazan refinery. OPEC+'s seven core producers held October quotas unchanged near 31.01m bpd at their 6 September meeting. Away from the war premium, non-oil momentum held up: UAE and Saudi PMIs stood at 55.3 and 53.8 respectively in August, both multi-month highs. Sources: Bloomberg, Al Jazeera.

C. Global Intelligence

US equities snapped a four-day losing streak Friday as August CPI matched forecasts: the S&P 500 rose 0.9% to 7,656.98, the Dow gained 1.0% to 52,573.29 and the Nasdaq added 1.0% to 26,333.04. Even so, a firmer core reading pushed fed funds futures odds of a 25bp hike at next week's 15-16 September FOMC meeting to roughly 86-90%, up from about 70-72% a day earlier, as the 10-year Treasury yield held near 4.95% after nearing 5% mid-week and the 2-year sat near 4.63%. Congress's stopgap funding bill already extends federal funding through 11 December, removing near-term shutdown risk. Gold held near $4,386/oz and silver eased to $64.90/oz as the weekend's oil-driven inflation shock reinforced the case for a hike heading into Fed week. Sources: CNBC, Advisor Perspectives.
