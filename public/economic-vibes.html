Daily Brief · Thursday, 19 November 2026 · Hawkish Fed & Gold Slide Edition

KSE-100 drops 1,223 points (−0.69%) to 176,294 amid broad sector selling; hawkish FOMC minutes drag S&P 500 to 5th straight loss and sink gold 3.1% to $3,737; Nvidia reverses −2.89% post-beat; SBP Governor urges export-led growth

Markets digested hawkish October Federal Reserve minutes flagging lingering inflation risks and keeping rate hikes on the table. On Wall Street, the Dow fell 1.11% on a managed-care rout (UNH −10%+, Humana ~−13%), S&P 500 slipped 0.23%, and Nasdaq edged up 0.42% even as Nvidia reversed −2.89% despite its Q3 beat. Commodities sold off sharply on a firmer dollar (Brent $91.95, WTI $83.26, Gold $3,736.80, Silver $45.00). Domestically, USD/PKR firmed marginally to 276.55 while SBP Governor Jameel Ahmad highlighted rebuilt FX buffers above $18bn.

02Regional Market & Economic Matrix

A. Local Market — Pakistan Focus

Broad-based institutional selling pushed the KSE-100 closer to 176,000. The KSE-100 closed Wednesday at 176,294.33 (−1,223.41, −0.69%; previous close 177,517.74) after touching an intraday high of 177,719.05 and low of 176,053.64 (PSX, Investify) [V]. Volume contracted to 742.12m shares (Rs 25.97bn traded value, 392,180 trades) with negative breadth (179 advancers, 285 decliners, 103 unchanged); UBL, LUCK and MEBL led index drags while WTL topped volumes [V]. Macro & Policy: SBP Governor Jameel Ahmad stressed export-led growth and structural reform, noting FX buffers exceed $18bn; Finance Minister Aurangzeb outlined a Rs2trn SME financing goal by June 2028 (Mettis, Pakistan Today) [V]. Energy moves include transitioning Thar coal mines to the national grid ($30m/yr savings) and receiving a record 15,000-tonne LPG cargo [V]. FX & Money: USD/PKR closed at 276.55 interbank (1.18 paisa firmer) (Mettis) [V]; open market ~277.20/279.75. SBP policy rate 12.0%; reserves $21.57bn; Oct CPI 9.6% [V, carried].

B. GCC & Middle East

Saudi TASI softens past 10,200; IMF upgrades UAE outlook. The Saudi Tadawul All Share Index (TASI) closed Wednesday down 53.04 points (−0.52%) at 10,200.16 on turnover of SAR 3.24bn (Arab News) [V]. Despite maritime trade friction near the Strait of Hormuz, the IMF upgraded the UAE's economic outlook citing robust fiscal buffers and banking capitalization (Arabian Business) [V]. Regional Integration: GCC states are finalizing common customs and transit standards for the 1,768 km regional railway network (targeted for Dec 2030), alongside Saudi IPO underwriting and disclosure reforms [V].

C. Global Intelligence — USA, Fed & Commodities

Hawkish FOMC minutes & healthcare sell-off keep S&P 500 in the red; gold & oil slide. Wednesday Wall Street closed mixed: S&P 500 7,323.63 (−0.23%, 5th straight loss, below 200-day MA), Dow 50,198.17 (−1.11%), Nasdaq 24,429.89 (+0.42%); VIX dipped 1.14% to 25.17 (AP, Cboe) [V]. October FOMC minutes revealed hawkish concern over sticky inflation, while proposed federal Medicare Advantage audit rules hammered UNH (−10%+) and Humana (~−13%). Nvidia reversed an early pop to close −2.89% ($150.96) despite its Q3 beat, while AMZN (+3.82%) and TJX (+8%+) gained ahead of Anthropic's IPO [V]. Yields: 10Y 5.065% (−3.6 bps), 2Y 4.798% (+1.1 bps) (Morningstar) [V]. Commodities: Stronger USD and rising US inventories drove Brent to $91.95 (−1.28%), WTI to $83.26 (−3.2%), COMEX gold to $3,736.80 (−3.1%) and silver to $45.00 (−4.84%) (NDTV Profit, Kitco) [V].
