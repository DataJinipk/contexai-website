02Regional Market & Economic Matrix

A. Local Market — Pakistan Focus

Intraday gains evaporated as profit-taking dragged the KSE-100 below 178,000. The KSE-100 closed Tuesday at 177,517.74 (−665.21, −0.37%; previous close 178,182.95) after climbing to an intraday high of 179,450.96 and touching a low of 177,371.14 (Business Recorder, Dawn) [V]. Market volume stood at 837.1m shares with negative breadth (164 advancers, 251 decliners) [V]. Macro Support: SBP data showed Pakistan's current account posted a surplus of $39m in October 2026, bolstered by worker remittances (BR) [V]. Fuel prices cut by Rs2/litre (petrol Rs328, diesel Rs358) are now active (BR) [V]. FX & Money: USD/PKR closed at 276.56 interbank (Re0.01 firmer) (BR, Mettis) [V]; open market ~277.00/279.50. SBP policy rate remains 12.0%; foreign exchange reserves stand at $21.57bn [V, carried]. October CPI at 9.6% (Aaj) [V, carried].

B. GCC & Middle East

Gulf bourses track regional pause; Saudi edges down. The Saudi Tadawul All Share Index (TASI) closed Tuesday down 11.16 points (−0.11%) at 10,253.20 with 93 gainers and 165 decliners on turnover of SAR 3.52bn (Arab News) [V]; Nomu fell 0.93% to 21,696. Regional attention remains on Gulf energy flows and defense coordination after recent intercepts, while OPEC+ prepares for its 29 Nov meeting on 2027 output quotas [V]. Regulatory: UAE's mandatory energy efficiency standard for low- and large-capacity commercial air conditioners came into operational enforcement, mandating RFID tagging and higher performance thresholds (Adherent) [V].

C. Global Intelligence — USA & Oil

Nvidia beats the whisper numbers; relief rally sweeps futures. Tuesday regular session closed defensively: S&P 500 7,340.53 (−1.03%), Dow 50,759.42 (−1.64%), Nasdaq 24,328.38 (−0.74%) with the VIX spiking 11.13% to 25.46 (AP, Cboe) [V]. However, post-market trade was dominated by Nvidia's stellar Q3 report: revenue of $115.8bn (+103% YoY) crushed estimates, alongside $130bn Q4 guidance, driving Nasdaq 100 futures up ~1.0% in early Asian trade (Benzinga) [V]. The 10-year Treasury yield held at 5.10% (range 5.09–5.15%) (Trading Economics) [V]. Oil: Brent crude settled at $93.14 (+0.8%) and WTI at $86.01 (+0.3%) on Middle East tensions offsetting supply surplus projections (Rigzone) [V]. Gold: Spot gold was subdued at ~$3,845/oz on a resilient US dollar (Kitco, USAGold) [V].
