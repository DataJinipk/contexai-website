A. Local Market — Pakistan Focus

A fourth straight gain on earnings and institutional buying. The KSE-100 closed Thursday at 167,193.43 (+1,456.68, +0.88%, previous close 165,736.75), touching about 167,500 early before paring gains around midday. KSE-100 volume was ~366m shares; the broader market traded ~817m shares worth Rs31.5bn [EST., single source]. Business Recorder attributes the rally to corporate earnings and institutional buying, while Express Tribune cites banks, fertiliser and investment shares (Business Recorder, PakTribune) [V]; index-point contributors and breadth were not published. Rates: the SBP raised its policy rate 50bp to 12.0% on 26 Oct by a 9-1 vote (The News); 6M KIBOR was 12.39%/12.64% on 29 Oct (SBP file, single source) and September CPI was 10.3% (Profit) [V].
