Breadth stayed narrowly constructive — 226 of 494 traded names advanced against 221 declines and 47 unchanged — with automobile assemblers, cement, commercial banks and E&P leading sector buying as market capitalisation rose to Rs19.093 trillion. 02Regional Market & Economic Matrix

A. Local Market — Pakistan Focus

Pakistan's macro narrative pivots on the IMF mission that landed in Karachi and Islamabad on Tuesday to open the fourth EFF review and a long-delayed Article IV consultation — talks that will run roughly two weeks and directly test the Sovereign Wealth Fund (Amendment) Bill now before the Senate Standing Committee on Finance, alongside the Governance and Anti-Corruption Diagnostic reform plan. Daily Independent notes up to $1.2 billion is riding on a clean review. Equities held a narrow positive bias — the KSE-100 added 0.15% to 171,402.08 on breadth of 226 advancers to 221 decliners — as easing oil prices offset the external-account overhang from Pakistan's import bill, per Business Recorder. The State Bank held its policy rate at 11.5% for a fourth straight meeting on 14 September, with 6-month KIBOR at 11.98% and August CPI still running near 11.15% y/y, well above the 5-7% target band, per Trading Economics. Separately, the FBR's September rule amendments cut the super tax on large exporters to 8% from 10%, targeted relief the textile lobby has sought since the budget cycle, per Daily Pakistan.

B. GCC & Middle East

Gulf markets are digesting two parallel tracks: energy policy normalising and non-oil diversification accelerating. OPEC+ has now completed its full unwind of the 2023 voluntary cuts with a further 188,000 bpd hike in September, per CNBC, and the group is expected to hold quotas steady for the rest of 2026 barring a fresh shock. On the ground, non-oil momentum remains firm — the UAE's seasonally adjusted PMI climbed to a seven-month high of 54.2 in September from 53.3 in August, per S&P Global. In Saudi Arabia, the PIF board, chaired by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, approved its 2026-2030 strategy this month, restructuring holdings into Vision, Strategic and Financial portfolios and shifting emphasis from rapid deployment to return discipline, per PIF; the fund has also launched the Gulf Coast Development Company (7 September) and the Tawrid supply-chain financing platform (20 September) as Vision 2030 delivery vehicles.

C. Global Intelligence

Wall Street closed mixed Tuesday as the AI trade kept the Nasdaq at a record 27,244 (+0.5%) even as the Dow fell 0.36% to 51,864 and the S&P 500 was flat at 7,764.64, per Yahoo Finance. Treasury yields were little changed (10Y 4.96%, 2Y 4.74%) as markets digest the Fed's 16 September hawkish 25bp hike to 3.75-4.00% and await Governor Michael Barr's Wednesday remarks, per CNBC. The dominant catalyst is Beijing: Xi Jinping arrives in Washington Wednesday for his first US visit since 2015, ahead of a Thursday summit with President Trump covering trade, AI, critical minerals, Iran and Taiwan; Washington is reportedly holding off on new excess-capacity tariffs until after the meeting, with a roughly $30 billion Chinese purchase pledge in play, per Bloomberg.
