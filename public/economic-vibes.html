02Regional Market & Economic Matrix

A. Local Market — Pakistan Focus

A quiet, cautious session after a 2.5% weekly rebound. The KSE-100 closed Monday at 166,564.06 (+154.81, +0.09%; previous close 166,409.25), trading between 165,585.42 and 166,989.76. Regular-market volume was 443.4m shares worth Rs17.73bn (825m and Rs23.12bn prior); 137 advanced, 319 declined and 40 were unchanged of 496 traded (Business Recorder, ARY News) [V]. Business Recorder's Topline notes buying in select heavyweight commercial banks and cement, with profit-taking capping gains, as investors weighed domestic inflation against easing crude; sector point-contributions were not published. The index had gained 2.5% (+4,034 pts) in the week to 30 Oct, snapping a five-week losing streak, after shedding 3,560 points in October (Business Recorder, Dawn) [V]. Macro: October CPI eased to 9.6% y/y (10.3% prior; +1.1% m/m), the 4MFY27 average is 10.1% (Mettis); the SBP raised its policy rate 50bp to 12.0% on 26 Oct (Mettis); 6M KIBOR rose 2bp to 12.43%/12.68% and the 28 Oct T-bill cut-offs were 12.11% (1M), 12.43% (3M), 12.65% (6M) and 12.89% (12M) (Mettis) [V]. FX: USD/PKR closed 276.72 interbank (276.75 prior); open market 277.30/280.00 (Mettis) [V]. IMF: a staff-level agreement was reached (cited in the 26 Oct MPC statement); Board date and tranche size were not confirmed in sources read [EST.]. FBR missed its four-month target by Rs96bn (Dawn) [V].

B. GCC & Middle East

Saudi GDP still contracting, OPEC+ on hold, Hormuz risk on the tape. Tadawul's TASI was quoted at 9,882.85 on 2 Nov, up about 0.45% from 9,838.28 (down ~5.9% in the month, ~13.9% y/y) (Trading Economics) [EST., single source with conflicting daily-change fields]. Saudi Q3 preliminary GDP contracted 3.8% y/y (−4.7% in Q2; +1.3% q/q), with oil activities −20.4% and non-oil +1.9% (Trading Economics / GASTAT) [V]. OPEC+ agreed over the weekend to keep production targets unchanged through December (Trading Economics) [V]. Brent slid toward ~$108.6 as Iranian and Qatari officials held a third day of talks; Iran says it could reopen Hormuz within two days if the US eases its blockade, which Washington has not accepted, and three ships were reportedly hit in 72 hours (Kitco, Hawksmoor) [EST.]. The latest Saudi non-oil PMI (September) was 55.3 (Trading Economics); October's is due ~3 Nov. DFM/ADX closes and any PIF mandate dated this week were not found.
