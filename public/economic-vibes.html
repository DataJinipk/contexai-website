Daily Brief · Friday, 20 November 2026 · Wall Street Tech Rout & PSX Rebound Edition

KSE-100 rebounds +478 pts (+0.27%) to 176,772 as SBP reserves rise to $21.59bn; Wall Street tumbles (S&P −2.02%, Dow −1,101 pts, VIX 28.44) despite record $81.3bn Anthropic IPO; Nikkei plunges >3%

Global equities suffered a sharp risk-off wave on Thursday as tech and AI profit-taking overshadowed Anthropic's record-breaking $81.3bn Nasdaq debut (closed at $97.17 vs $83 IPO price). The S&P 500 fell 2.02% (6th straight loss), Nasdaq dropped 2.25%, Dow shed 1,101 points (−2.20%), and VIX jumped nearly 16% to 28.44. Gold slid further to $3,682.10 (−1.46%) while Brent held steady near $91.98. Domestically, the KSE-100 snapped its pullback (+0.27% to 176,772), USD/PKR firmed to 276.52, and SBP reserves rose $15m to $21.59bn.

02Regional Market & Economic Matrix

A. Local Market — Pakistan Focus

KSE-100 stabilizes above 176,700 as SBP reserves edge higher. The KSE-100 closed Thursday at 176,772.18 (+477.85, +0.27%; previous close 176,294.33) after testing an intraday high of 177,929.61 and low of 176,596.77 (PSX, Investify) [V]. Ready-market volume printed 723.4m shares (Rs 21.13bn traded value) with mildly positive breadth (214 advancers, 205 decliners, 24 unchanged) [V]. Macro & Policy: SBP FX reserves increased by $15m w/w to $21.587bn (total liquid reserves $26.775bn) (Mettis) [V]. FinMin Aurangzeb chairs today's ECC meeting on a 6-point agenda (including PAPCO throughput claims and supplementary grants), while Power Division clarified industrial base tariffs remain unchanged despite CPPA's Rs 2.88/unit October FCA filing (ProPakistani, ARY News) [V]. FX & Money: USD/PKR closed at 276.52 interbank (3.09 paisa firmer; range 276.50–276.55) (Mettis) [V]; open market ~277.00–277.24 / 278.35–279.50. SBP policy rate 12.0%; Oct CPI 9.6% [V, carried].

B. GCC & Middle East

Saudi TASI dips on banking profit-taking; IMF sees UAE rebounding 7.3% in 2027. The Saudi Tadawul All Share Index (TASI) closed Thursday down 41.26 points (−0.40%) at 10,158.90 on turnover of ~SAR 3.0bn (156m shares), weighed by Al Rajhi and SNB ahead of the weekend (Arab News) [V]. Following its 2026 Article IV mission, the IMF upgraded UAE real GDP growth to +0.2% in 2026 and 7.3% in 2027, citing swift policy agility, well-capitalized banks, and resilient logistics despite Strait of Hormuz friction (IMF, Arabian Business) [V].

Broad Wall Street sell-off hits S&P 500 for 6th day; VIX spikes to 28.44. Thursday Wall Street slid across the board: S&P 500 7,175.83 (−2.02%), Dow 49,097.06 (−2.20%, −1,101.11 pts), Nasdaq 23,879.20 (−2.25%); VIX jumped 15.8% to 28.44 (Morningstar, Cboe) [V]. Anthropic (ANT) completed the largest US IPO in history ($81.3bn at $83/sh), opening at $111.50 before profit-taking pulled it to $97.17 (+17.1%), while Nvidia fell −3.30% ($145.98) [V]. Yields eased on haven flows: 10Y ~5.04%, 2Y ~4.77% (Morningstar) [V]. Commodities: COMEX gold slid 1.46% to $3,682.10/oz and silver fell 1.51% to $44.32/oz; Brent settled at $91.98 (+0.03%) and WTI at $83.26 (Morningstar) [V].
