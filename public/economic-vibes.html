02Regional Market & Economic Matrix

A. Local Market — Pakistan Focus

Pakistan opens August after a turbulent week in which an oil-price shock — crude up more than 20% in July on Strait-of-Hormuz tensions — dragged the tape mid-week before Friday's rebound. The KSE-100 closed Friday at 176,094 (+0.31%, +546 pts), a slim green finish that steadied nerves after sharp swings, with commercial banks, investment companies, cement and textiles doing the heavy lifting and breadth positive at 62:36. The domestic anchors remain constructive: the SBP held the policy rate at 11.50% for a third meeting, June CPI eased to 11.1%, and the IMF framework keeps external visibility intact — though July inflation is expected to tick up toward ~9.1% as the energy pass-through bites. The swing factor is imported: sustained high oil lifts the import bill and imported-inflation risk, while a firmer US dollar caps rupee room. With rates anchored near 278 and earnings season live, the market is consolidating with an upward bias — provided oil cools.

B. GCC & Middle East

OPEC+ leans into supply as the Gulf's non-oil engine carries growth. With Hormuz exports recovering, OPEC+ approved a further output increase for August — the fifth consecutive monthly hike as the group keeps unwinding 2023-era cuts (Rigzone, Al Jazeera). Saudi Arabia's August target rises to ~10.42m bpd, a supply cushion that should temper the July price spike into autumn. The non-oil pivot remains the structural story: Saudi non-oil PMI printed 53.3 (a four-month high) while the UAE eased to 50.8 amid regional disruption, with activity expected to accelerate through H2 (AGBI). PIF-anchored Vision-2030 mega-projects — NEOM, Red Sea, Diriyah, New Murabba — keep the diversification deal pipeline open for Gulf-focused sponsors and Pakistani corporates.

C. Global Intelligence

US equities shut July with momentum — the Nasdaq +1.0% to 25,374, the S&P 500 +0.7% to 7,490, and the Dow +0.53% to 52,485, its fourth straight winning month, powered by an Amazon-led mega-cap rally on strong cloud earnings (Washington Post/AP). But the rates picture stays firm-for-longer: the 10-year sits at ~4.75% and the 2-year at ~4.28% after the Fed's hawkish hold, keeping the dollar bid and precious metals soft — gold ~$4,054, silver ~$58.26 (both −1%) (Advisor Perspectives). The set-up into August: resilient earnings versus a Fed in no hurry to cut and an oil market whipsawed by Gulf geopolitics — a mix that keeps rate-sensitive and EM assets on a tighter leash.
