02Regional Market & Economic Matrix

A. Local Market — Pakistan Focus

The KSE-100 slipped 199.84 points (0.11%) to close at 176,966.69 on Monday, swinging between an intraday high of 178,122.88 and a low of 176,735.57 as profit-taking in ENGROH, MEBL, SRVI, SYS and MCB (−453 points combined) offset gains in OGDC, PPL, MLCF, DGKC and FCCL (+392 points); volume was healthy at 930m shares worth Rs44bn, led by CNERGY (266m shares), with breadth modestly negative (222 gainers vs 241 losers of 498 traded). The session followed Moody's upgrade of Pakistan's sovereign rating to B3 from Caa1 (stable outlook) on governance gains and easing external risk — FX reserves near $17bn versus $14bn a year earlier, and interest payments down to ~35% of revenue from 49% — extending the momentum from S&P's B upgrade on 22 July. July CPI eased to 9.2% y/y from 11.1% in June, the SBP held its policy rate at 11.5% for a second straight meeting, and KIBOR sat near 11.39/11.89% as of 20 August; an IMF mission is expected in September for the next EFF/RSF review, with roughly $1.2bn in view.

B. GCC & Middle East

Brent fell 2.5% to $92.06/bbl and WTI dropped to $84.89/bbl even as Washington unveiled its toughest-ever Iran sanctions package, with Tehran warning of possible Strait of Hormuz ship seizures in response. Seven OPEC+ members continued a fifth straight 188,000 bpd monthly output increase, while Saudi Arabia's non-oil private sector expanded at a slightly softer pace in July (Riyad Bank PMI 53.1, down from 53.3 in June). Separately, PIF's 2026-2030 strategy, published 12 August, deepens its tilt toward tourism, entertainment and private-sector co-investment, on the heels of its completed Electronic Arts acquisition with Silver Lake and Affinity Partners on 4 August — the diversification anchor even as Hormuz-linked shipping risk clouds the near-term oil outlook.

C. Global Intelligence

Wall Street was split Monday — the Dow added 0.3% to 53,417.16 while the S&P 500 slipped 0.3% to 7,652.86 and the Nasdaq fell 0.8% to 25,980.19 as chip stocks sold off (Micron −5.8%, AMD and Broadcom down more than 3% and 2%); Treasury yields eased, with the 10-year near 4.72% and the 2-year near 4.19%, ahead of Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's first Jackson Hole address on Friday, 28 August — nineteen days before the 16 September FOMC decision, where markets are pricing roughly one-in-three odds of a hike after the 9-3 hold on 29 July. Washington simultaneously rolled out its toughest-ever sanctions regime against Iran — "Operation Economic Outcast", per Treasury Secretary Bessent — while Canada vowed retaliatory tariffs after President Trump threatened a 50% levy on Canadian autos, parts and steel. Gold held firm near $4,652-4,657/oz and silver near $69/oz as havens stayed bid into the policy-heavy week.
