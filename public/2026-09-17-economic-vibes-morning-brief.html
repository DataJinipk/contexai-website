01Five Things You Need To Know To Start Your Day

  • PSX Selloff The KSE-100 fell 1,370.52 points (−0.81%) on Wednesday to close at 168,021.80, giving back most of Tuesday's reserves-driven bounce. Breadth turned decisively negative — 320 of 496 traded names declined against just 121 advancers — on volume of 356.14 million shares worth Rs19.49bn. Topline Securities attributed the selling to "escalating Middle East tensions and elevated oil prices" prompting institutions to lock in gains, with FFC, ENGRO, HBL and LUCK the heaviest drags while MLCF (+2.28%) and PPL (+1.27%) bucked the trend. Business Recorder and Daily Independent confirm the print. Sentiment: profit-taking on geopolitics.
  • Fed Delivers First Hike Since 2023 The FOMC voted 12-0 to raise the federal funds target range by 25bp to 3.75%-4.00%, its first increase since 2023, as Chair Kevin Warsh flagged persistent inflation risk. The updated dot plot now points to a median 4.1%-4.4% by year-end 2026 — up from the prior 3.6%-4.1% — signalling a higher-for-longer stance despite the hike. CNBC and FXStreet. Sentiment: hawkish surprise.
  • Wall Street Sinks Post-Fed US equities sold off into the decision: the Dow dropped 631.21 points (−1.21%) to 51,461.90, the S&P 500 fell 0.45% to 7,551.81, and the Nasdaq slipped 0.01% to 25,978.42. The 10-year Treasury yield touched a fresh 52-week high of 5.041% intraday before settling near 4.97%; the 2-year spiked to about 4.64%. CNBC and TheStreet. Sentiment: risk-off.
  • Oil Retreats on Pipeline Repair Brent settled at $105.83/bbl (−2.7%) and WTI at $102.43 (−3.2%), pulling back from multi-month highs after US officials said Saudi Arabia's drone-damaged East-West pipeline would resume operations within days, easing the acute phase of the Hormuz-linked supply scare. OPEC+'s core seven nonetheless held October output unchanged at their September 6 meeting given the disruption's persistence. Per CNBC and Energy Connects. Still-elevated crude keeps Pakistan's import bill a live current-account risk. Sentiment: premium easing, not gone.
  • Gold & Silver Rally on Hike Day Gold rose 1.07% to about $4,338/oz and silver jumped 1.65% to roughly $64.70, with safe-haven and inflation-hedge demand outweighing the drag of a hawkish Fed as investors focused on the dot plot's higher-for-longer inflation implications. Per Trading Economics and Yahoo Finance. Sentiment: safe-haven bid.

02Regional Market & Economic Matrix

A. Local Market — Pakistan Focus

Wednesday's 0.81% PSX pullback to 168,021.80 erased most of Tuesday's reserves-driven bounce, with breadth swinging sharply negative (320 decliners vs 121 advancers) as Topline Securities cited "macroeconomic stability and external account" concerns tied to elevated oil prices and Middle East tensions. The State Bank held its policy rate at 11.5% at its September 14 MPC meeting even as August CPI ran hot at 11.15% y/y, per Trading Economics, well above the 5-7% target band; 6-month KIBOR sits near 11.86% per SBP data. The rupee held broadly steady around 277.70 in the interbank market, per Business Recorder. Pakistan and the IMF are set to open talks around September 22-23 on the fourth EFF and third RSF reviews, alongside the country's first Article IV consultation in two years, with roughly $1.2bn in combined disbursement at stake and Islamabad reportedly on track to meet six of seven quantitative targets, per 24NewsHD and TechJuice.

B. GCC & Middle East

Oil's Hormuz/pipeline risk premium eased but did not disappear: Brent and WTI both fell more than 2.5% Wednesday after US officials signalled Saudi Arabia's damaged East-West pipeline would restart within days, per CNBC. Even so, OPEC+'s core seven held October output policy unchanged at their September 6 meeting, wary of Gulf supply that remains partly shut in. Non-oil momentum stays firm — Saudi Arabia's PMI hit 53.8 in August, a six-month high and fifth straight month of expansion, while the UAE's composite reading rebounded sharply to 55.3 from 52.7, per Arab News. Riyadh's PIF kept executing its 2026-2030 strategy, launching the Gulf Coast Development Company on September 7 to build an integrated coastal destination at Al-Khafji in the Eastern Province, per PIF.

C. Global Intelligence

The Fed delivered its first rate hike since 2023, lifting the funds target range 25bp to 3.75%-4.00% on a unanimous 12-0 vote, with Chair Kevin Warsh citing persistent inflation risk; the updated dot plot points to a higher-for-longer 4.1%-4.4% median by year-end, per CNBC. Equities sold off into the news — Dow −1.21%, S&P 500 −0.45%, Nasdaq roughly flat — while the 10-year Treasury brushed a fresh 5.04% high before settling near 4.97%. Separately, Congress returned to Washington for a brief pre-midterm session with renewed pressure to regulate AI, but per Semafor the legislative window is closing fast, with the House set to adjourn around September 17 and lawmakers turning to the November 3 midterm campaign.
