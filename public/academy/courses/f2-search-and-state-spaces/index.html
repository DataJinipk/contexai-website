Search, State Spaces and Why Symbolic Methods Survive

Search is the oldest idea in AI and the one most often written off as obsolete. It is not: planning loops, constraint solving and verifier-based reasoning are all search, and CS2023 deliberately retains it. You will implement the algorithms rather than read about them.

What you will be able to do

On successful completion, you will be able to:

  • Construct a state-space representation of an informal problem.
  • Explain why the choice of state representation dominates performance.
  • Implement breadth-first search over a state space.
  • Compare BFS, DFS and uniform-cost search on completeness and optimality.
  • Explain what makes a heuristic admissible and why it matters.
  • Evaluate whether a proposed heuristic preserves optimality.

How it works

Every module is taught aloud, one line at a time, with a pause after each line so it lands before the next one arrives — and you can go back, skip or replay at any point. Each module ends with a coding exercise graded by automated tests: you pass when your code does.

On completion you receive a ContexAi Academy Certificate of Completion. It records what you can demonstrably do; it is not an accredited qualification.
