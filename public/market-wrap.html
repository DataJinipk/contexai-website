01Executive Dashboard — how the session played against the morning view

Morning view vs outcome. This morning's brief was Cautious: 176,731 was the first floor, the KSE-100 had closed Friday under the 178,008 pivot after a run of lower highs, and Asia was softer ahead of Nvidia's results. The session opened above the pivot (178,315.62) and buying lifted the index to 179,692.98 by early afternoon, through R1 (179,150) by 543 pts and above Friday's 179,284 high, so the run of lower highs ended. It did not reach R2 (180,427). Gains then faded, and Business Recorder reported profit-taking towards the end of the session; the index touched 178,065.73 in the closing minutes and settled at 178,182.95 (+309.08, +0.17%), 175 pts above the morning pivot. S1 was never tested. Net: a better day than the morning read allowed for, but the late sell-off from above R1 keeps the call at Cautious rather than constructive.
