01Executive Dashboard — how the session played against the morning view

Morning view vs outcome. This morning's brief was Cautious, with support at 176,327, a 178,319 pivot and resistance at 179,366 (desk reference levels, estimates). The session ran almost exactly inside that frame. The index dipped to 176,865.05 early, holding 538 pts above S1. It then rallied on banks, E&Ps, OMCs and refineries, helped by Mari Energies' copper-gold discovery in Chagai, and peaked at 179,284.34 in the second session, 82 pts short of R1. It gave back ~1,410 pts into the close to finish at 177,873.88 (+500.11), 445 pts under the morning pivot. The index closed neither at the morning support zone nor back above the morning pivot.
