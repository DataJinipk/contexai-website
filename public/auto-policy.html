01Five Things That Changed This Week

  • IMF: No Objection (Reported)The Express Tribune reports the PM approved the Autos and Auto Parts Manufacturing Policy 2026–31 after the IMF raised no objection to its draft. Dawn reported the same on 8 Nov; on 7 Nov the PM's adviser said fiscal provisions were still subject to IMF agreement (PakWheels). There is still no IMF statement or Finance Ministry text, and the reports do not say whether the EV sales-tax tiers were part of what was cleared. Desk moves its IMF status from "not cleared" to "reported no-objection, unverified". Express Tribune (via Investify) · Dawn · PakWheels · REPORTED, UNVERIFIED
  • Targets Get NumbersThe $6bn export goal is now $6.01bn: $3.29bn from parts makers and $2.72bn from vehicle makers. Existing makers must lift local value addition 2% a year, with 2030–31 floors of 40% for cars/SUVs, 80% for tractors and 90% for motorcycles and rickshaws. A Rs2.1bn vendor technology-upgrade programme and a local-tax drawback for exporters are included. Express Tribune (via Investify) · DRAFT-REPORTED
  • The Market Is Already VotingOctober passenger-car sales reached 21,635 (+60% YoY); 4MFY27 is ~63,500 (+48%; 63,507 per Pakistan Observer, 63,418 per Business Recorder). The Suzuki Alto alone sold 11,363, about 52.5% of all cars, while Indus Motor (Toyota) fell 10% and Honda Atlas 19% YoY (company totals, Topline). September was depressed by hybrid sales-tax uncertainty, so the monthly jump flatters momentum. Daily Pakistan · Pakistan Observer · ProPakistani (Topline) · Business Recorder · Bol News · V
  • Route: Cabinet, Then a Money BillThe tax measures, including the Automotive Development Levy (ADL) on ICE vehicles above 1,000cc, are expected through a mini-budget via a money bill after cabinet approval. The ADL rates (2% for 1,001–2,000cc, 10% above, per drafts) and its reported FY27–31 yield of Rs188.58bn (ProPakistani 21 Oct) remain DRAFT-REPORTED. Express Tribune (via Investify) · ARY News · Dawn · DRAFT-REPORTED
  • Finance Got DearerThe SBP raised the policy rate 50bp to 12% from 27 Oct (9 of 10 votes), citing the Middle East conflict and elevated global energy prices; October CPI was 9.61%. The policy's proposed larger EV loans (Rs10m, 5 years) still need an SBP regulatory change. Next MPC: 14 Dec. SBP MPS 26 Oct · EconoTimes · PakWheels · V

04Reality Check — Desk Verdicts

Unchanged by this week's news. $6bn exports: Implausible — the desk's base case is about $2.0bn cumulative (range $1.4–3.4bn, EST., uncalibrated); even the upside reaches about 57% of the target. 30% EV share by 2030: Implausible (a Stretch for FY31–32 if reforms deliver). $17.7bn FX saving: Implausible as a net figure. Revenue neutrality: Stretch.
New question 1 — does the car floor bite? The proposed 40% localisation floor for cars sits below the ~58% average localisation estimated for passenger cars (Profit, Apr 2026). Either the policy measures value addition differently, or the floor will not constrain existing makers. The definition matters more than the number.
New question 2 — is vendor support sized to the job? Parts makers are asked to deliver $3.29bn of exports. The support line reported so far is Rs2.1bn, against a projected levy of Rs188.58bn (ProPakistani 21 Oct) and a desk estimate of Rs34.8–56.5bn a year of vendor revenue displaced by the tariff glide and used imports (EST., placeholder-driven).
Mood: CAUTIOUS — the direction is clearer and the IMF hurdle looks lower, but nothing is notified and the money bill will decide who pays.
