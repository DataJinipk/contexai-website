KSE-100 grinds higher, Wall Street hits a record, and oil slumps on Hormuz signals — your Tuesday, 22 September 2026 Economic Vibes brief.

THE OVERNIGHT TAPE
S&P 500 +1.49% to 7,765, Nasdaq +2.26% to a record 27,122, Dow +0.71% to 52,049 — Wall Street's best session since early August on an AI-led rally. The 10-year Treasury eased to 4.96% (2-year 4.74%) even as the Fed's September 16 hike to 3.75%-4.00% — its first since 2023 — keeps markets pricing at least one more increase this year. Brent fell 3.67% to $100.06 and WTI dropped 4.69% to $95.59 on tentative signs of US-Iran de-escalation around the Strait of Hormuz. Gold eased 0.48% to $4,360; silver held near $66.90.

PAKISTAN
The KSE-100 added 268.58 points (+0.16%) to close at 171,153.16 in a volatile session — an early 800-point advance faded by the close — on turnover of 692.88 million shares (Rs20.10bn) and narrowly positive breadth (238 advancers vs 213 decliners). LUCK, ATRL, FFC, MARI and EFERT led index contributors. The SBP held its policy rate at 11.5% on September 14 even as August CPI accelerated to 11.1% y/y; the rupee held firm at 277.22 interbank. The IMF mission lands in Islamabad today for the fourth EFF review, third RSF review and a first Article IV consultation in two years, with the Senate reviewing a Sovereign Wealth Fund (Amendment) Bill 2026.

GCC
OPEC+ has held its October output target unchanged amid Strait of Hormuz disruption, with the next review due October 4. Saudi Arabia's non-oil PMI held at a six-month high of 53.8 in August while the UAE's gauge has cooled toward breakeven. Saudi Arabia's PIF has approved its 2026-2030 strategy, prioritizing tourism, urban development, advanced manufacturing, logistics, clean energy and Neom.

GLOBAL
Chinese President Xi Jinping arrives in Washington September 23-25 for talks on trade, Taiwan and AI with President Trump. Congress's stopgap funding law signed September 2 keeps federal agencies funded through December 11, removing near-term shutdown risk.

LEGISLATIVE TRACKER
Pakistan: Sovereign Wealth Fund (Amendment) Bill 2026 before the Senate Finance Committee — wins for governance/transparency, added oversight costs for SWF-held entities.
GCC: OPEC+ holds October output steady — wins for Gulf producers and PIF's funding runway, loses for oil-importing economies.
Global: Trump-Xi Washington summit and a hawkish Fed — wins for risk assets into the summit, loses for rate-sensitive sectors.

THE DAY AHEAD
KSE-100 support 170,900 (Monday's session low; deeper support at 170,000), pivot 171,153 (Monday's close), resistance 171,730 (Monday's intraday high; then 172,500). Our desk favors selective accumulation near support on IMF-review clarity, with tight stops given the unresolved Hormuz standoff. All levels are indicative desk estimates, not recommendations.
