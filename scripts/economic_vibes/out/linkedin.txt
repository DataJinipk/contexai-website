KSE-100 sheds 1,693 points to 173,636 as renewed Iran-Kuwait strikes and refinery penalties spook investors ahead of a hawkish Fed's 16 September decision

Good morning — here's the ContexAi Research desk's read on markets for Tuesday, 08 September 2026, covering Monday's close.

THE OVERNIGHT TAPE
US markets were shut Monday for Labor Day, so Friday's hot-jobs-driven selloff carries into today's open: the S&P 500 fell 0.38% to 7,718.60, the Dow lost 0.51% to 53,414.25 and the Nasdaq eased 0.29% to 26,506.99, with the 10-year Treasury at 4.78% and the 2-year at 4.37%. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh sharpened his inflation warning at Jackson Hole on 28 August, and CME FedWatch now prices roughly a 58–60% probability of a hike at the 16 September FOMC. Gold eased 0.5% to $4,405/oz and silver held near $66.90 as the dollar firmed. Brent traded near $97.3/bbl and WTI near $92.4/bbl as Hormuz tensions stayed elevated.

PAKISTAN
The KSE-100 shed 1,692.75 points (0.97%) to close at 173,636.07 on Monday, swinging from an intraday high of 175,354 to a low of 173,604. Volume fell to 679.19 million shares worth Rs23.69bn, with breadth turning decisively negative at 154 advancers to 311 decliners. UBL, OGDC, PPL, MCB and FFC dragged the index down roughly 811 points as renewed Iran-Kuwait strikes, a firmer oil tape and fresh refinery-sector penalties tied to the 1 October upgrade deadline weighed on sentiment. August CPI held at 11.1% y/y, the SBP's policy rate stays at 11.5%, and USD/PKR interbank held near 277.40. The IMF's fourth EFF review mission is expected this month.

GCC
The UAE's non-oil PMI rose to 55.3 in August, its fastest pace since December 2024, while Saudi Arabia's PMI climbed to a six-month high of 53.8, both resilient despite the conflict backdrop. OPEC+'s seven core members held October quotas steady at their 6 September meeting, sticking with their roadmap into year-end while preparing a capacity assessment for 2027 targets. Saudi's PIF launched the Gulf Coast Development Company on 7 September to deliver an integrated coastal destination at Al-Khafji, part of its board-approved 2026–2030 strategy under Vision 2030.

GLOBAL
Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole pivot has reset rate expectations just as markets had priced in room to ease, lifting hike odds for the 16 September FOMC toward 60%. The other swing factor remains the Middle East, where continued US-Iran strikes — including a 5 September US strike on the Iranian tanker M/T Kylo — keep Hormuz risk elevated and Brent within reach of $100/bbl.

LEGISLATIVE TRACKER
• Pakistan: Refiners face escalating penalties for missing the 1 October deadline to sign upgradation agreements under the brownfield refinery policy.
• GCC: OPEC+ held October quotas steady at its 6 September meeting, prepping a capacity assessment for 2027 targets.
• Global: The Fed's 16 September FOMC meeting looms with hike odds near 58–60% after Chair Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole warning.

THE DAY AHEAD
KSE-100 — Support 173,600 (deeper support 172,500) · Pivot 173,636 (Monday's close) · Resistance 175,350 (Monday's intraday high, then 176,000).

Desk stance: Monday's heavy-cap drag from UBL, OGDC, PPL, MCB and FFC against thin offsetting gains, falling volume and a 3-to-2 negative breadth ratio point to genuine distribution.
