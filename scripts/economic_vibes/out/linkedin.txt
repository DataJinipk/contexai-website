KSE-100 edges higher as Wall Street posts its best session in weeks and gold spikes on renewed Iran war fears

Good morning — here's the ContexAi Research desk's read on markets for Friday, 04 September 2026, covering Thursday's close.

THE OVERNIGHT TAPE
The S&P 500 jumped 1.06% (+81.00) to 7,747.71, the Dow surged 623.57 points (1.18%) to 53,686.11 and the Nasdaq climbed 1.40% (+366.23) to 26,584.06 — the strongest session in weeks — as the 10-year Treasury yield eased to 4.77% off its multi-year high and the 2-year held near 4.35%. Fed Governor Christopher Waller struck a dovish tone, saying he could "sit and be patient" as inflation cools, tempering but not eliminating the roughly 66% odds CME FedWatch assigns to a hike at the 16 September FOMC meeting. Gold surged 3.5% to near $4,489/oz and silver reclaimed $67/oz as renewed US-Iran hostilities kept haven demand bid even as equities rallied. Brent held near $95.5/bbl and WTI near $91.3/bbl, little changed.

PAKISTAN
The KSE-100 inched up 153.09 points (0.09%) to close at 174,929.68 on Thursday, recovering a slice of Wednesday's 1,690-point rout. Ready-market volume was 627.45 million shares worth Rs25.19bn, with breadth improving to 252 advancers vs 207 decliners. OGDC, PACE, TPL and TELE led gainers while Pakistan Refinery, Bank Makramah and Cnergyico lagged, with Topline Securities flagging renewed rotation into autos, cement and E&P. August CPI is running near 11% y/y, and the SBP is expected to hold its policy rate at 11.5% at the 14 September MPC; USD/PKR interbank was little changed near 277.52. The Sovereign Wealth Fund (Amendment) Bill 2026 remains before the Senate Standing Committee ahead of the IMF's 4th EFF and 3rd RSF reviews, due to open this month.

GCC
Oman denied Iran's claim that the two had agreed Strait of Hormuz transit fees for shipping, while President Trump threatened to bomb Oman if it "gets in the way" of talks to restore commercial passage — a reminder of how fragile any de-escalation remains as the conflict enters its seventh month. OPEC+ completed its 188,000 bpd September output increase on 2 August and heads into a 6 September meeting expected to hold quotas steady into Q4. Saudi Arabia's non-oil PMI matched a six-month high of 53.8 in August, while the UAE's held at 55.3, its fastest pace since December 2024.

GLOBAL
Wall Street's best session in weeks came alongside a gold spike — a reminder that dovish Fed signals and lingering Hormuz tail risk are being priced simultaneously. Equity and haven-asset gains on the same day suggest investors see easier policy ahead even as the Gulf conflict stays unresolved.

LEGISLATIVE TRACKER
• Pakistan: Sovereign Wealth Fund (Amendment) Bill 2026 remains before the Senate Standing Committee ahead of September's IMF reviews.
• GCC: OPEC+ heads into its 6 September meeting expected to hold quotas steady into Q4.
• Global: The Fed's 16 September FOMC meeting looms with hike odds near 66% even as yields ease and Waller turns dovish.

THE DAY AHEAD
KSE-100 — Support 174,900 (deeper support 174,000) · Pivot 174,930 (Thursday's close) · Resistance 175,800 (Thursday's intraday high, then 176,467).

Desk stance: Thursday's gain looks like tentative stabilization rather than a durable reversal — breadth improved but volume actually fell versus Wednesday, so conviction remains thin. A calmer oil tape eases Pakistan's import-bill math into the SBP's 14 September MPC, but a stronger dollar-gold bid and 66% Fed hike odds argue against near-term PKR strength. We favour a selective tilt toward E&P, banks and IMF-programme beneficiaries while watching the 6 September OPEC+ decision, the MPC, and any fresh Hormuz escalation.
