KSE-100 rebounds 575 points on energy-led buying as gold breaks above $4,600 and Wall Street closes out a volatile week higher

Good morning. Here's the Economic Vibes desk read for Monday, 24 August 2026.

THE OVERNIGHT TAPE
Wall Street closed higher Friday -- S&P 500 +0.4% to 7,674.37, Dow +1.0% to 53,277.01, Nasdaq +0.4% to 26,180.45 -- but all three indices still logged a weekly loss after a bond-market selloff pushed the 10-year Treasury yield back up to 4.74% and the 2-year to roughly 4.18%, as the relief from the US Treasury's mid-week buyback announcement faded and Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish policy tilt stayed unmoved.

PAKISTAN
The KSE-100 rebounded 574.76 points (0.33%) to close at 177,166.52 on Friday, recovering from an intraday low of 176,110.75 as value-hunting in oil & gas names -- Attock Refinery (+5.09%), OGDC (+1.99%), Hub Power (+1.25%) -- offset drag from Thal Limited, United Bank and The Bank of Punjab. Breadth turned positive (246 gainers vs 216 losers) on volume of 689.2m shares worth Rs35.66bn. July CPI eased to 9.2% y/y and SBP held its policy rate at 11.5% for a second straight meeting; an IMF mission is expected in September for the next EFF/RSF review, with roughly $1.2bn in view.

GCC
Brent held near $94.4/bbl and WTI near $87.3/bbl as Strait of Hormuz shipping disruption kept a risk premium in crude, even as OPEC+ continues raising output by 188,000 bpd a month. Saudi Arabia's non-oil private sector held a fourth straight month of expansion in July (PMI 53.1) even after Q2 real GDP contracted 4.8% y/y -- the kingdom's first quarterly contraction since 2023 -- while Kuwait's non-oil PMI rebounded to 50.8. Separately, PIF published its 2026-2030 strategy, pivoting to private-sector-led "value realisation" in tourism, entertainment and advanced manufacturing.

GLOBAL
Gold climbed to a three-month high near $4,603/oz (+~1.9%) on renewed fiscal-sustainability hedging even as Treasury yields stayed firm, with silver near $68.97/oz. The Treasury's buyback-driven yield relief proved short-lived, keeping the tension between fiscal yield-management and Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish stance in focus ahead of the next FOMC meeting.

LEGISLATIVE TRACKER
- Pakistan: SBP holds at 11.5% as CPI eases to 9.2%; September IMF mission in view for ~$1.2bn -- wins for depositors and exporters if the tranche clears, headwind for leveraged borrowers awaiting a cut.
- GCC: OPEC+ output hike plus PIF's 2026-2030 strategy amid Hormuz disruption and a Saudi GDP contraction -- wins for Gulf budgets and PIF-linked tourism names, headwind for the broader Saudi economy and shippers/insurers.
- Global: Treasury's buyback relief fades as yields climb back toward Warsh's hawkish backdrop -- wins for gold and haven assets, headwind for the Fed-independence narrative and risk assets.

THE DAY AHEAD
KSE-100 support 176,100 (Friday's intraday low) -- pivot 177,167 (Friday's close) -- resistance 178,000, then 180,000. We read Friday's rebound as constructive but narrow, concentrated in oil & gas names rather than broad-based conviction, arguing for a cautiously constructive stance while watching the 176,100-177,200 band.
