KSE-100 sheds 1,464 points as the Hormuz standoff and a hawkish Fed repricing dominate the tape.

THE OVERNIGHT TAPE
US equities closed lower Tuesday — S&P 500 −0.24%, Dow −0.16%, Nasdaq −0.55% — as tech weakness and stalled US-Iran Strait of Hormuz talks weighed on sentiment. The 10-year Treasury yield pushed above 4.7%, its highest since January, with markets now pricing roughly 55% odds of a 25bp September Fed hike — a sharp reversal from July's split 9-3 hold. Brent and WTI both surged ~5% to settle near $87.72 and $82.13 on fears a naval blockade keeps Hormuz constrained. Gold reclaimed $4,400/oz and silver hit a seven-week high near $65.50 on safe-haven demand.

PAKISTAN
The KSE-100 retreated 0.81% (−1,464 pts) to 179,846.68, giving back ground after touching an intraday high of 181,017 as banks, cement, fertiliser, autos, E&P and OMCs all came under selling pressure. SBP held its policy rate at 11.5% for a second straight review even as July CPI cooled to 9.2% from 11.1% (food inflation, notably, accelerated to 10.6%). USD/PKR interbank held broadly steady at 277.67. Islamabad is preparing for a fifth IMF EFF/RSF review mission next month that could unlock roughly $1.2bn, with energy circular debt and privatisation progress the key tests.

GCC
Seven OPEC+ members lifted output by 188,000 bpd for August, a fifth straight monthly increase, even as Hormuz risk keeps prices elevated. Saudi Arabia's non-oil PMI held firm at 53.1 in July (from 53.3), with Kuwait's reading rebounding. The PIF, chaired by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, approved its 2026–2030 strategy, pivoting toward value creation with tourism, travel and entertainment as priority sectors under Vision 2030.

GLOBAL
The Hormuz standoff and a hawkish Fed repricing are now the two swing factors for global risk sentiment. Washington's new demands on Tehran have clouded any near-term reopening of the strait, keeping oil bid and inflation risk — and hence rate-hike odds — elevated into September's FOMC.

LEGISLATIVE TRACKER
• Pakistan: 5th IMF EFF/RSF review due next month (~$1.2bn at stake) — wins: banks, FX reserves, sovereign risk; loses: energy consumers facing tariff conditionality.
• GCC: OPEC+ +188,000 bpd for August; PIF's 2026–2030 strategy backs tourism/entertainment — wins: oil-importing economies, Saudi non-oil diversification plays; loses: OPEC+ producers' per-barrel revenue.
• Global: Fed repricing toward a 25bp September hike (~55% odds) — wins: USD, fixed-income savers; loses: rate-sensitive equities, EM FX including the PKR.
