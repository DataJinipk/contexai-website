KSE-100 grinds higher on narrow breadth as the IMF lands in Islamabad and Xi lands in Washington — 23 September 2026

THE OVERNIGHT TAPE
Wall Street closed mixed: the S&P 500 was flat at 7,764.64, the Dow fell 0.36% to 51,864 on drag from Cisco, JPMorgan and American Express, while the Nasdaq notched a fresh record at 27,244 (+0.5%) on continued AI-trade strength. Treasury yields held steady (10Y 4.96%, 2Y 4.74%) as markets digest the Fed's hawkish 16 September hike to 3.75-4.00%. Brent (-1.0% to $99.25) and WTI (-1.2% to $94.59) eased after Trump told the UN Iran would "make a deal." Gold slipped 0.8% to $4,307.63 and silver eased to $65.30 on dollar strength.

PAKISTAN
The KSE-100 added 248.92 points (+0.15%) to close at 171,402.08 on narrow breadth (226 advancers, 221 decliners) and lighter turnover of 641.83m shares (Rs18.47bn). Banks, cement, autos and E&P led sector buying. The headline event: an IMF mission landed in Karachi and Islamabad to open the 4th EFF review and a first-in-two-years Article IV consultation, with the Sovereign Wealth Fund (Amendment) Bill and governance benchmarks under scrutiny — up to $1.2bn is riding on a clean outcome. SBP held its policy rate at 11.5% for a fourth straight meeting; August CPI ran near 11.15% y/y, still well above the 5-7% target band; 6-month KIBOR sits at 11.98%. The FBR's September rule change also cut the exporter super tax to 8% from 10%.

GCC
OPEC+ has completed its unwind of 2023's voluntary output cuts with a further 188,000 bpd September hike and is expected to hold quotas steady into year-end. UAE non-oil PMI climbed to a seven-month high of 54.2. Saudi Arabia's PIF board, chaired by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, approved its 2026-2030 strategy, restructuring into Vision, Strategic and Financial portfolios, with the Gulf Coast Development Company and Tawrid supply-chain platform launched this month as delivery vehicles.

GLOBAL
Xi Jinping arrives in Washington Wednesday — his first US visit since 2015 — ahead of Thursday's summit with President Trump on trade, AI, critical minerals, Iran and Taiwan. Washington is reportedly holding new excess-capacity tariffs until after the meeting, with a roughly $30bn Chinese purchase pledge in play.

LEGISLATIVE TRACKER
Local — Pakistan: Sovereign Wealth Fund (Amendment) Bill under IMF review. Wins: governance-compliant SOEs, bondholders. Loses: SOE managements facing tighter disclosure.
GCC: PIF 2026-2030 strategy. Wins: Vision Portfolio giga-projects, private co-investors. Loses: legacy holdings facing rationalisation.
Global: Trump-Xi summit tariff delay. Wins: Chinese exporters, US agriculture/aircraft. Loses: US steel and metals producers.

THE DAY AHEAD
KSE-100 support 170,867 (Tuesday's intraday low; deeper support near 169,500) · pivot 171,402 (Tuesday's close) · resistance 171,681 (Tuesday's intraday high, then 172,500). The desk reads the tape as cautiously constructive: narrow breadth argues for positioning ahead of the IMF review outcome rather than broad conviction. A clean signal from Karachi/Islamabad is the key near-term catalyst; a stumble there, or renewed oil volatility, would quickly pressure the rupee.
