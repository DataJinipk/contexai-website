KSE-100 rebounds 1,422 points on record SBP reserves — but the real story today is the Fed, with hike odds surging past 85% and the 10-year Treasury at its highest since 2007.

THE OVERNIGHT TAPE
Wall Street softened into decision day: S&P 500 −0.45% to 7,585.73, Dow −0.63% to 52,093.11, Nasdaq −0.78% to 25,981.57. The 10-year Treasury yield briefly touched 5.04% intraday, its highest since 2007, before settling near 5.01%; the 2-year sits at 4.39%. CME FedWatch now prices roughly 87-90% odds of a 25bp hike at today's 2pm ET decision — a sharp swing from the coin-flip odds of just weeks ago, driven by hot inflation and a resilient jobs report.

PAKISTAN
The KSE-100 gained 1,421.67 points (+0.85%) on Tuesday to close at 169,392.33, recovering most of Monday's Hormuz-driven selloff. Breadth was strongly positive — 280 of 494 names advanced against 166 decliners — led by UBL, FFC, LUCK, BAFL and PSEL. The rally tracked the State Bank's FX reserves hitting an all-time high of $21.4bn on Eurobond inflows, already surpassing the December 2026 target. The rupee held steady at 277.30 interbank. SBP's policy rate stays at 11.5% despite August CPI at 11.15% y/y. Pakistan and the IMF open talks around September 22 on the fourth EFF and third RSF reviews plus the first Article IV consultation in two years, with roughly $1.2bn in combined disbursement at stake.

GCC
Brent rose to about $107.50/bbl, a four-month high, and WTI to $102.69, after Saudi Arabia reportedly cancelled export shipments following drone strikes that shut its East-West pipeline. OPEC+ completed its rollback of voluntary output cuts, adding ~188,000 bpd in September before a planned Q4 pause. Non-oil momentum holds — GCC composite PMI at 51.5 for a 21st straight month above 50, Saudi at 56.6, UAE at 54.2. Saudi's PIF continues its 2026-2030 strategy pivot toward commercial returns ahead of the September 28-30 Mega Projects Summit in Riyadh, expected to showcase a $200bn+ pipeline.

GLOBAL
Gold slipped about 1% to a six-week low near $4,270/oz and silver eased to roughly $62.82 as a firmer dollar and the looming Fed decision pulled traders to the sidelines. In Washington, Congress returned from recess with funding secured through December 11 but no consensus on AI regulation, leaving just a handful of legislative days before the November 3 midterms.

LEGISLATIVE TRACKER
• Pakistan: IMF's 4th EFF/3rd RSF review (~Sep 22) + FBR's Sep 30 filing deadline — wins for banks and sovereign credit on record reserves; loses for importers facing a wider oil bill.
• GCC: PIF's 2026-2030 strategy + OPEC+ cut rollback complete — wins for logistics/mining/AI allocations and oil exporters; loses for giga-project contractors under PIF's commercial-return pivot.
• Global: Fed's Sep 16 decision (hike priced ~87-90%) as Congress punts on AI rules — wins for AI incumbents facing no near-term guardrails; loses for rate-sensitive growth stocks and long-duration bonds if the hike is confirmed.
