KSE-100 snaps its rally — down 1,733 points (−1.01%) to 170,498.94 — as global yields hit a 19-year high and oil holds above $106 on the still-shut Strait of Hormuz.

THE OVERNIGHT TAPE
US equities were mixed-to-lower Thursday: S&amp;P 500 ▼0.06% to 7,704.23, Dow ▼0.31% to 51,349.89, Nasdaq ▼1.1% to 26,936.04. The 10-year Treasury yield climbed to roughly 5.11-5.12%, its highest since 2007, while the 2-year rose to 4.94%, as Fed Governor Barr and President Williams flagged further tightening under Chair Kevin Warsh. October rate-hike odds jumped to 70-77% on CME FedWatch.

PAKISTAN
The KSE-100 fell 1,733.57 points (−1.01%) to 170,498.94, ending a three-session rally, on broad-based selling (358 decliners vs 101 advancers) across autos, cement, banks, E&amp;P and OMCs. Volume eased to 759.97m shares but value rose to Rs27.54bn. The pullback came as an IMF mission opened talks on the next EFF/RSF review and PM Shehbaz Sharif met MD Kristalina Georgieva at UNGA. August CPI held at 11.15% y/y (still above the SBP's 5-7% band); the SBP kept its policy rate at 11.5% on 14 September; 6-month KIBOR sits at 11.72/11.97%. The Senate Standing Committee on Finance and Revenue deferred the Sovereign Wealth Fund (Amendment) Bill, 2026, seeking a clause-by-clause justification.

GCC
Saudi Arabia's PMI rose to a six-month high of 53.8 in August (from 53.1); the UAE's non-oil PMI jumped to 55.3 (from 52.7), its fastest pace since December 2024 — both resilient despite the Strait of Hormuz remaining effectively closed to routine shipping (IMF PortWatch: 1 transit on 20 September vs an 85/day baseline). PIF continued rolling out its 2026-2030 strategy, with CEER's flagship vehicle launch on 21 September. OPEC+'s JMMC meets 4 October to review production policy.

GLOBAL
Brent rose 3.2% to $106.39 and WTI gained 2.8% to $94.76, up roughly 22% over the past month, on the ongoing Hormuz disruption. Gold eased 0.3% to $4,273 and silver fell 1.7% to $63.36 as a firmer dollar and hawkish Fed repricing pressured metals. Washington's tariff agenda widens 29 September: 100% Section 232 duties on patented pharmaceuticals extend to all remaining importers, alongside expanded Section 338 tariffs on Canadian goods.

LEGISLATIVE TRACKER
Pakistan: Sovereign Wealth Fund (Amendment) Bill deferred in Senate committee — wins: institutional investors, IMF compliance; loses: SOE management facing tighter oversight.
GCC: OPEC+ JMMC meets 4 October amid Hormuz shut-ins — wins: non-oil diversification plays; loses: export-constrained Gulf energy exporters.
Global: Section 232/338 tariffs widen from 29 September — wins: onshoring pharma manufacturers, generics; loses: mid-size branded importers, Canadian exporters.

THE DAY AHEAD
KSE-100 support 169,500 (deeper support 168,700), pivot 170,499 (Thursday's close), resistance 171,700 (then Thursday's intraday high of 172,491). The desk reads Thursday's move as profit-taking after a three-session rally rather than a reversal, though a 19-year-high 10-year yield, surging October hike odds and Brent above $106 argue for a cautious open.
