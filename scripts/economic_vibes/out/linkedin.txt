KSE-100 rebounds 1,021 points to 169,043 as falling oil calms import-bill fears — and Wall Street snaps back post-Fed even as the dot plot still flags one more hike.

THE OVERNIGHT TAPE
US equities reversed Wednesday's hawkish-Fed selloff: the S&P 500 climbed 1.14% to about 7,637, the Nasdaq jumped 1.69% to roughly 26,418, and the Dow rose 0.62% to near 51,779, with technology leading and only communication services and financials lagging. The 10-year Treasury eased over 7bp to about 4.93% from a fresh 52-week high near 5.02%, though the Fed's September dot plot still points to one more 25bp hike this year to a 4.00%-4.25% range by year-end.

PAKISTAN
The KSE-100 gained 1,021.40 points (+0.61%) on Thursday to close at 169,043.20, clawing back most of Wednesday's pullback. The All-Share Index rose 0.68% on 383 million shares traded market-wide, with exploration & production, commercial banks and cement the top sectoral contributors as overnight oil weakness eased import-bill and inflation concerns. The State Bank held its policy rate at 11.5% at its September 14 meeting despite August CPI at 11.1% y/y; 6-month KIBOR sits near 11.86%. The rupee held broadly steady near 277.37 interbank. Pakistan and the IMF open talks around September 22 on the fourth EFF and third RSF reviews plus the first Article IV consultation in two years, covering revenue collection, energy-sector viability and governance reforms.

GCC
Brent settled at $104.82 (−$1.01) and WTI at $101.91 (−$0.52), extending Wednesday's slide as US officials reiterated Saudi Arabia's damaged East-West pipeline is on track to restart within days. OPEC+ has signalled it expects to hold output steady through year-end after completing its planned September restoration. Non-oil momentum stays firm — Saudi PMI hit a six-month high of 53.8, UAE's composite reading rose to 55.3, its strongest since late 2024. PIF advanced its 2026-2030 strategy, having launched the Gulf Coast Development Company on September 7 for an Al-Khafji coastal project.

GLOBAL
Gold rebounded 1.12% to about $4,310.80/oz and silver jumped 2.94% to roughly $65.52, recovering ground surrendered right after Wednesday's hike as bargain buyers returned. In Washington, the House adjourned Wednesday until November without acting on AI regulation, leaving the issue to simmer through the November 3 midterms despite bipartisan pressure to legislate before the recess.

LEGISLATIVE TRACKER
• Pakistan: IMF's 4th EFF/3rd RSF review + first Article IV consultation (~Sep 22) — wins for banks/sovereign credit on a further disbursement path; loses for energy/revenue sectors facing fresh IMF scrutiny.
• GCC: OPEC+ signals steady output through year-end + PIF's Gulf Coast Development Company — wins for oil exporters and PIF-backed tourism/logistics; loses for oil-importing economies still absorbing higher landed costs.
• Global: Fed's hawkish dot plot (one more 2026 hike) as Congress adjourns without AI rules — wins for the dollar and short-duration savers; loses for long-duration bonds, rate-sensitive growth names and child-safety AI advocates.
