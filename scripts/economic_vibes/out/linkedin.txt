KSE-100 sheds 2,541 points as Hormuz escalation meets a near-5% Treasury yield — but Tuesday's record FX reserves print is already clawing back the losses.

THE OVERNIGHT TAPE
Wall Street softened Monday: S&P 500 −0.48% to 7,619.98, Dow −0.29% to 52,421.20, Nasdaq −0.56% to 26,186.41. The real story was rates — the US 10-year briefly topped 5% intraday for the first time since 2023 before settling near 4.99%, while the 2-year climbed to 4.66%. Wednesday's FOMC decision is now a coin flip: J.P. Morgan expects a 25bp hike, Goldman Sachs calls one "very unlikely."

PAKISTAN
The KSE-100 fell 1.49% (−2,541 pts) to 167,970.66 on Monday as fresh Houthi and Iranian attacks in the Gulf triggered broad de-risking — only 83 of 492 names advanced. Tuesday opened with relief buying, up nearly 2,000 points, after the State Bank's FX reserves hit an all-time high of $21.4bn on Eurobond inflows. SBP holds its policy rate at 11.5% for a third straight meeting despite August CPI at 11.15% y/y. The IMF's fourth EFF review mission lands September 23, with roughly $1bn in tranche disbursement at stake.

GCC
Brent settled at $108.15/bbl, a four-month high, and WTI at $102.64, after Oman postponed Gulf-Iran Hormuz talks and Saudi Arabia's drone-damaged East-West pipeline stayed shut. OPEC+ continues gradually restoring output even as the crisis lifts prices. Non-oil momentum holds — GCC composite PMI at 51.5, Saudi at 56.6, UAE at 54.2. Saudi's PIF approved its 2026-2030 strategy, pivoting from giga-project scale toward logistics, mining and AI infrastructure ahead of the September 28-30 Mega Projects Summit in Riyadh.

GLOBAL
Gold eased about 1% to $4,310/oz and silver slid to roughly $63.30 as rate-hike odds firmed and the dollar strengthened. In Washington, Congress returned from recess with funding secured through December 11 but no consensus on AI regulation, leaving a 29-state patchwork in place with just four legislative days left before the November 3 midterms.

LEGISLATIVE TRACKER
• Pakistan: IMF's 4th EFF review (Sep 23) + Finance Bill 2026 tax reforms — wins for salaried taxpayers and banks; loses for importers facing a wider bill from the oil shock.
• GCC: PIF's 2026-2030 strategy + OPEC+ output normalisation — wins for logistics/mining/AI allocations; loses for giga-project contractors facing write-downs.
• Global: Congress punts on AI rules; FOMC decision Sep 16 — wins for AI incumbents facing no near-term guardrails; loses for rate-sensitive growth stocks if the Fed leans hawkish.
