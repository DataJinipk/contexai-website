KSE-100 slips as Hormuz tension flares and the Fed stays hawkish — Economic Vibes, 29 September 2026

Monday's tape was a reminder that the biggest swing factor for Pakistani markets right now isn't local — it's oil and US rates.

THE OVERNIGHT TAPE
The KSE-100 opened the week up as much as 273 points before fading to close at 170,425.62, down 339.60 points (−0.20%), as selling in autos, cement, banks, fertiliser, E&P and OMCs offset the early rally. Breadth turned negative (267 decliners vs 181 advancers) on volume of 421.01m shares and Rs17.74bn traded value.

Wall Street opened lower too: S&P 500 −0.77% to 7,683.69, Dow −0.70% to 51,481.51, Nasdaq −0.92% to 26,820.38, as the 10-year Treasury yield hit a fresh 19-year high near 5.21% and the 2-year touched 4.94%. Gold and silver each fell over 3% — even havens aren't immune to a rate shock.

PAKISTAN
SBP held its policy rate at 11.5% for a third straight meeting even as CPI inflation returned to double digits at 11.1% YoY in August. The IMF has begun its fourth EFF review and third RSF review, with roughly $1.1bn riding on the Sovereign Wealth Fund (Amendment) Bill now before the Senate.

GCC
OPEC+'s core members held October output at September levels after unwinding the 1.65mn bpd voluntary cut tranche. Non-oil PMIs stayed strong into August (Saudi 53.8, UAE 55.3), and PIF kept deploying Vision 2030 capital — Tawrid supply-chain financing, CEER's flagship vehicles, and the Gulf Coast Development Company in Al-Khafji.

GLOBAL
A stopgap bill signed 2 September funds the US government through 11 December, averting an October 1 shutdown — but the real story is oil: Brent's jump toward $105 after Trump rejected Iran's Hormuz proposal revived inflation fears just as the Fed, under Chair Kevin Warsh, leans toward another hike on 28 October.

LEGISLATIVE TRACKER
🇵🇰 Pakistan: SWF Amendment Bill — wins: governance/IMF credibility; loses: discretionary control over SOE assets.
🌍 GCC: 15% Pillar Two top-up tax — wins: GCC treasuries; loses: in-scope multinationals.
🇺🇸 Global: US funding stopgap to 11 Dec — wins: federal contractors; loses: duration-sensitive equities facing another Fed hike.

THE DAY AHEAD
KSE-100 support at 169,500 (deeper at 168,000), pivot at 170,425, resistance at 171,150. The desk reads Monday's dip as risk-off driven by oil and rates, not a local growth story — but a hawkish Fed and a Hormuz-driven oil spike are the levels to watch.
