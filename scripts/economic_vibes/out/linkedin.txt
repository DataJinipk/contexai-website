KSE-100 rebounds 641 points as the KP ratification clears the NA and Senate, while a hot US CPI print sends Wall Street and precious metals sharply lower.

THE OVERNIGHT TAPE
US stocks sold off Wednesday after September CPI came in hotter than expected at 3.7% y/y (vs 3.6% forecast) with core CPI at 2.6% y/y: the Nasdaq lost 1.88% to 26,692.87, the S&P 500 fell 1.11% to 7,718.94 and the Dow slipped a milder 0.09% to 51,621.48. Treasury yields whipsawed before easing (10Y 5.19%, 2Y 4.82%), while December Fed hike odds jumped to roughly 83-89% from ~72% a day earlier. Gold settled at $4,120.82 (-1.0%) and silver slid 5.9% to $55.70 as the repricing hit precious metals hardest.

PAKISTAN
The KSE-100 reversed two sessions of selling, climbing 641.07 points (+0.39%) to close at 165,417.51, after the National Assembly and Senate both ratified the federal emergency proclamation in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa on Wednesday (PTI staged a walkout in protest), clearing the way for today's 11:30am joint sitting to formally impose Governor's Rule under Article 234. Buying in ENGROH, LUCK, FFC, BAHL and NBP added 381 points to the index on turnover of roughly 586 million shares worth Rs 20.1 billion. The rupee held flat at 276.95 interbank, the SBP policy rate remains at 11.5% ahead of its 26 October review, September CPI sits at 10.3% y/y, and the $1.2bn IMF tranche still awaits Executive Board sign-off.

GCC
Saudi Arabia's Tadawul All-Share Index fell 1.74% to 10,138.83 on broad-based selling (252 decliners vs 15 advancers), with the Nomu parallel market off 0.90%, even as OPEC+ kept November output quotas unchanged at a combined 31.01 million bpd and Brent held near $105.43 with WTI near $95.17. The UAE's VAT Executive Regulation amendments remain in force, continuing to weigh on SME compliance costs even as the non-oil diversification story stays intact.

GLOBAL
Washington remains funded under the continuing resolution through 11 December, pushing the next shutdown risk past the midterms. The hot CPI print has markets debating whether the Fed delivers a double hike or a 50bp move in December, with odds now near 83-89% for at least one hike.

LEGISLATIVE TRACKER
- Pakistan: NA/Senate ratify the KP emergency; today's joint sitting formalises Governor's Rule under Article 234 - the federal government and PPP gain, PTI and KP's provincial autonomy lose.
- GCC: OPEC+ holds November output steady + UAE VAT rules stay in force - oil producers and compliant corporates win; UAE SMEs and broader Gulf sentiment (Tadawul -1.74%) lag.
- Global: Hot CPI pushes December hike odds to ~83-89% while the CR funds the government through 11 December - near-term fiscal certainty and cash holders win; rate-sensitive equities and precious metals lose.

THE DAY AHEAD
KSE-100 support 164,060 (deeper support 163,000 if today's vote disappoints), pivot 165,417 (Wednesday's close), resistance 165,629 then 167,089 if the KP vote clears cleanly. Our stance: lean constructive into today's joint sitting, favour the names that already led Wednesday's rally (banks, fertilisers, refineries) on confirmed follow-through, and treat a clean break above 165,629 as confirmation rather than a reason to chase pre-vote.
