KSE-100 surges 1,841 points to a record 170,885 on broad-based buying — while Wall Street closes a volatile Fed-hike week mixed and gold clears $4,350 ahead of Pakistan's IMF review.

THE OVERNIGHT TAPE
US equities closed a volatile week quietly: the S&P 500 edged up 0.17% to 7,650.50 and the Nasdaq gained 0.39% to 26,522.55, while the Dow slipped 0.18% to 51,682.64 — its third straight losing week and worst since March. The 10-year Treasury climbed to about 5.01% as markets priced a roughly 58% chance of another 25bp Fed hike at the October 28 meeting, following Wednesday's first increase in three years to 3.75%-4.00%.

PAKISTAN
The KSE-100 gained 1,841.39 points (+1.09%) on Friday to close at a record 170,884.58, with breadth decisively positive — 351 advancers versus 110 decliners — on roughly 165.8 million KSE-100 shares traded. Automobile assemblers, cement, commercial banks, exploration & production and OMCs led, with LUCK, MCB, MEBL, NBP, UBL, MARI, OGDC, POL, PPL and PSO all in the green. The State Bank held its policy rate at 11.5% at its September 14 meeting despite August CPI at 11.1% y/y; 6-month KIBOR sits near 11.98%. The rupee held steady near 277.25 interbank. An IMF mission is due in Islamabad around September 22-23 for the fourth EFF review, third RSF review and the first Article IV consultation in two years; Pakistan has reportedly met six of seven end-June performance targets, with tax-net broadening the lone laggard.

GCC
Brent eased to $103.87 (−0.91%) and WTI fell 1.6% to $100.30, extending the week's pullback as Saudi Arabia's damaged East-West pipeline nears full repair. OPEC+ confirmed it will pause its monthly output increases for October, holding the group's combined target at 31.1 million bpd, with the next review set for October 4. Non-oil momentum stays firm — Saudi PMI held at a six-month high of 53.8, UAE's composite reading climbed to 55.3, its strongest since late 2024.

GLOBAL
Gold climbed 0.60% to about $4,368.60/oz, touching a weekly high near $4,440, while silver jumped 3.02% to $67.18, extending the post-Fed rally as easing oil dragged yields off their highs. In Washington, Congress's stopgap funding law signed September 2 keeps the government open only through December 11, setting up the next fiscal cliff just as the Fed weighs an October hike.

LEGISLATIVE TRACKER
• Pakistan: IMF's 4th EFF/3rd RSF review + first Article IV consultation (~Sep 22-23) — wins for banks/sovereign credit on a ~$1bn disbursement path; loses for tax-exposed sectors facing fresh scrutiny on the stalled tax-net benchmark.
• GCC: OPEC+ pauses October output increase, holding at 31.1m bpd — wins for Gulf producers defending price levels; loses for oil-importing economies in Asia.
• Global: Fed's Oct 28 meeting carries ~58% hike odds as Congress's CR funds government only through Dec 11 — wins for the dollar and short-duration savers; loses for long-duration bonds and rate-sensitive growth names.
