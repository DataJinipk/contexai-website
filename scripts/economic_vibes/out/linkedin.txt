🌅 Economic Vibes — Morning Brief | Friday, 07 August 2026

The KSE-100 stretched its rally to a fourth straight session while Wall Street cooled off from record highs and oil snapped back on renewed Hormuz uncertainty — here's what the desk is watching this morning.

THE OVERNIGHT TAPE
Dow -0.85% to 53,885 (pulling back after five record closes this week), S&P 500 -0.18% to 7,709.96, and Nasdaq -0.06% to 26,348.35, as rising Treasury yields (10Y 4.61%, 2Y ~4.18%) and a jump in oil prices weighed on sentiment even as softer ADP jobs data kept September rate-cut hopes alive.

PAKISTAN
KSE-100 gained 0.98% (+1,761.66 pts) to close at 181,776.59, a fourth straight advance on breadth-confirmed buying — 75 advancers vs 25 decliners among index names — led by banks, oil & gas E&P, cement, fertiliser and power on turnover of 345.78m shares (Rs40.31bn). CPI eased to 9.2% YoY in July from 11.1% in June, and the SBP held its policy rate at 11.5% for a second straight meeting; six-month KIBOR tracks near 11.70–11.90% (desk est.). USD/PKR ticked up modestly to 278.15 interbank.

GCC
OPEC+ agreed on 2 August to add a further 188,000 bpd from September. Saudi's non-oil PMI held near 53.1 in July while the UAE's climbed to a four-month high of 52.7. Saudi's PIF board approved its 2026-2030 strategy, pivoting from rapid project launches toward localisation, Saudisation and long-term industrial capability. Oil reversed higher — Brent +4.9% to $83.34, WTI +3.37% to $77.75 — after Iran tied any Hormuz reopening to the US lifting its naval blockade.

GLOBAL
Gold climbed to a seven-week high near $4,271 (+2.38%), and silver added 0.6% to $61.83, as haven demand stayed bid despite this week's record US equities. Washington's trade agenda keeps moving: a 50% Section 338 tariff on ~$20bn of Canadian dairy, alcohol and motor-vehicle exports lands in 12 days (19 August), while Pakistan's own reciprocal tariff with the US sits at a comparatively favourable 19%.

LEGISLATIVE TRACKER
Pakistan's Finance Act 2026 digital-creator withholding tax (5%/10%) continues to be deducted at source by banks, with a new IMF-linked tax-audit manual due by end-August.
Saudi's PIF 2026-2030 strategy shifts capital toward localisation even as Section 301 forced-labor duties keep Gulf exporters exposed.
The Canada tariff step-up (19 August) and Pakistan's 19% US tariff rate reshape relative trade competitiveness across textiles, dairy and autos.

THE DAY AHEAD
KSE-100: support 180,015 (Wednesday's close, deeper at 178,500), pivot 181,777 (Thursday's close), resistance 182,000 (psychological, then the 2026 high of 189,167 set in January).
Desk view: breadth-confirmed buying argues for a healthier tape, but oil's reversal on Hormuz conditions is the key swing factor for Pakistan's import bill and the disinflation narrative. All levels are indicative desk estimates, not recommendations.
